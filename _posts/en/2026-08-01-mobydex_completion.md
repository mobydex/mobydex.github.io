After three years of research and development, Moby Dex is reaching its conclusion. The project has developed a data-driven platform that systematically analyzes mobility supply and mobility demand, creating a new foundation for needs-based and sustainable mobility planning.

The project results will be presented at the official final event on Thursday, August 13, from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM, hosted by CAS Software AG in Karlsruhe.

In addition to presentations and project result showcases, participants can look forward to a special highlight: live demonstrations of the prototypes developed during the project. The various use cases and tools of the Moby Dex ecosystem will be presented and demonstrated in a practical setting. Visitors will gain insights into innovative approaches for analyzing mobility supply and demand, supporting regional mobility planning, and enabling corporate mobility management.

The event provides an opportunity to explore the project outcomes, engage with the project partners, and gain a deeper understanding of the potential of data-driven mobility solutions to support the mobility transition.

We look forward to an exciting final event in Karlsruhe.
